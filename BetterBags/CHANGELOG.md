BetterBags Next adds guild bank support, a compact movable window mode, and in-window bulk selling confirmations.

## New

- **Guild bank support in BetterBags Next**: BetterBags Next now replaces Blizzard's guild bank window with a matching categorized panel. View all tabs together in categories or inspect individual tabs in slot order, and drag categories between your backpack and the guild bank to deposit or withdraw, with a confirmation card when depositing into tabs you cannot withdraw from.
- **Compact small mode in BetterBags Next**: Toggle compact, movable bag panels using the minimize button on the backpack header. Small panels remember their screen positions, animate smoothly when resizing, and snap together when dragged alongside each other.

## Improved

- **In-panel bulk selling confirmation at vendors**: Selling a category of more than 12 items to a vendor now docks a confirmation card directly inside the vendor window instead of showing a separate dialog. Double-click the card to complete the sale, or right-click to cancel.
- **Search box visibility in BetterBags Next**: Search boxes across Next panels now feature a lighter background, lavender accents, and higher-contrast text. In addition, typed text in the vendor search box no longer overlaps with the search placeholder.
